## 🤖 Schema Generator
The **Schema Generator** is an AI-powered utility integrated into the Brightstar Cave Admin Terminal.

### **Instruction:**
Describe your application and data requirements in natural language. Gemini AI will automatically analyze your prompt and generate:
1.  **GraphQL Schema**: Defining tables, relationships, and constraints.
2.  **SQL DDL**: PostgreSQL-compatible scripts for table creation.
3.  **Vector Embeddings**: Configurations for semantic search capabilities.

**Example Prompts:**
- *"Build a luxury apartment booking system with availability calendars and guest profiles."*
- *"Create a member-only lounge access system with tiered membership levels."*
